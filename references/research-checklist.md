# Research checklist (search, then cite in the footer)

Run these as parallel WebSearch calls; re-check anything date-sensitive (visa regimes, rates, closures).

- **Entry**: visa / visa-free rules for the traveller's citizenship, validity dates, max stay, passport validity, arrival cards, onward-ticket checks.
- **Money**: exchange rate (central bank) and the cash rate; whether the traveller's bank cards work there (Russian cards usually don't abroad); local payment apps and whether foreigners can link cards; cash acceptance; tipping.
- **Connectivity**: blocked services (e.g. Google/Telegram/WhatsApp in China), roaming vs local SIM vs eSIM, VPN needs.
- **Flights**: direct routes, typical round-trip price for the season, baggage rules of likely carriers (carry-on vs checked on return).
- **Weather** for the travel month; daylight/sunset time if a sunset activity is planned.
- **Closures**: weekly museum closing days, public holidays and big events that spike prices (trade fairs, auto shows).
- **Transport**: airport → centre options and prices, metro fares, ride-hailing apps, intercity trains and booking (passport needed?).
- **Sights**: opening hours, reservation requirements (some museums need free online booking), ticket prices and upgrade tiers.
- **Food**: local specialities, a fine-dining place that fits the route and the cap, verified local institutions (age, signature dish).
- **Shopping/customs**: tax-free threshold and refund rate, cash declaration limits both ways, home-country duty-free import limits, banned imports (e.g. meat products).
- **Practical**: plugs/voltage, safety notes, emergency numbers, apps to install, phrases in the local language(s) and script.
